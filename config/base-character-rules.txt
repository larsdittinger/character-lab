BASE CHARACTER ONLY — mandatory modeling constraints:
No cape, cloak, mantle, poncho, trailing fabric, long coat tails, hanging tabard or skirt panels. No backpack, quiver, weapons, shield, staff, bow, belt pouches, dangling straps, chains, banners, wings, decorative spikes, oversized protruding armor, or held accessories. Use fitted clothing and compact armor close to the body. Keep arms, legs, neck and torso silhouettes clearly separated and visible in every view. Natural anatomy such as ears stays part of the character; do not remove it. Keep hair compact and off the shoulders and back silhouette.
All optional equipment and loose cloth must be designed and modeled separately, in separate accessory references/assets, then attached to the finished rig. Do not include separate accessory drawings in this base-character sheet. If another part of the prompt requests forbidden equipment or loose cloth, omit that equipment from this image.
